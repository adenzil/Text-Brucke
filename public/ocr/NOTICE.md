# Offline image recognition

Textbrücke bundles the Tesseract.js 7.0.0 worker and Tesseract.js Core 7.0.0 WebAssembly engine, both under Apache-2.0. Their license files are included in the extension during the build. [Tesseract.js](https://github.com/naptha/tesseract.js/tree/v7.0.0) · [Tesseract.js Core](https://github.com/naptha/tesseract.js-core/tree/v7.0.0)

The German and English fast recognition data come from tessdata_fast 4.1.0, revision `65727574dfcd264acbb0c3e07860e4e9e9b22185`. The upstream files are compressed here with `gzip -n`. [Model sources](https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tessdata_fast/tree/65727574dfcd264acbb0c3e07860e4e9e9b22185) · [Apache-2.0 license](LICENSE.tessdata_fast) · [Checksums](SHA256SUMS)
